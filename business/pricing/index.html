FAQ

Pricing questions

Do prices include GST?

No. GST at 18% is added at checkout, and every payment gets a GST invoice.

How do I pay?

By card or UPI through Razorpay, with UPI Autopay for monthly plans. Scale and above are invoiced and set up with our team.

Can I change plans?

Yes, move up any time. Moving from a signal plan to a dashboard plan replaces it.

What happens when the trial ends?

If you haven't chosen a plan, your company drops to Free. You keep your saved locations.
