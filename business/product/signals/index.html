Signal capturing

A palette of 18 signals, each one traceable.

We collect public data on banking, tax, vehicles, satellite night-lights, digital payments and more, join it to each pincode, and combine it into purchasing power, income and spend.

Signals measured at district or state level are the same for every pincode inside that area. The purchasing-power model combines them with pincode-level ones; it doesn't pretend they are pincode measurements.

How a score is built

From public data to a pincode score

Collect

Pull each source in its published form, keep its licence terms, and record when it was fetched.

Join

Attach each value to pincodes using India Post's PIN boundaries and directory.

Model

Combine the signals into purchasing power, then estimate income and spend, calibrated to the national household survey.

Check

A weekly refit must pass fixed sanity gates (for example, known affluent areas must outrank their neighbours) before anything changes.

FAQ

About the data

Are these real incomes?

No. They are modelled estimates built from public data. They are good for comparing areas, not for judging a single household.

How often does it change?

The model is refitted weekly. Each source updates on its own schedule; some publish yearly.

Can I add my own data?

Yes. Upload your store list with revenue in the workspace. It stays in your account, encrypted, and is used to score your stores and calibrate your forecast.

Which signals come with which plan?

The three core metrics and the property-rate estimate are free. Signals Lite adds ten signals for ₹200 a month, and Signals Pro or any dashboard plan includes all of them.
