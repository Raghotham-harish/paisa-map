Post-login

What you can do once you're in

Insight

Location intelligence on every click

Click any pincode in the map workspace: opportunity score, how it compares with its district, state and neighbours, estimated income and spend, and the signals behind it.

  • Colour the map by your project's suitability
  • Distance rings around a site
  • Your own stores drawn on the map

Comparison

Compare up to five sites

Pick candidates on the map and compare them on opportunity, economics, income and spend, with a one-line verdict on which leads and why.

  • Ranked cards with suitability and risk
  • Bars for each metric
  • Save the shortlist to the project in one click

Pipeline

Every candidate, with a status

Saved locations move through shortlist, reviewing, approved and rejected, with notes your team can see.

Your data

Your stores, scored against their areas

Upload a CSV or Excel file with store, pincode and revenue. Each store gets a score, and the forecast calibrates to how your stores actually perform.
