Before and after you sign in

Before sign-in Free

For anyone curious about an area.

  1. Open the mapNo account needed.
  2. Search a city or pincodeOr let the map find where you are.
  3. Read the scoresPurchasing power, income, spend and the property-rate estimate.
  4. Switch viewsBubbles, choropleth, heatmap, clusters, bivariate, top-N.
  5. Sign in with GoogleWhen you want to save locations.

  4. Compare and forecastShortlist sites and turn a budget into a plan.
  5. ShareInvite your team, add free viewers, send a report.
