FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

Where do the numbers come from?

From public sources: the national household spending survey, RBI banking data, income-tax filer counts, vehicle registrations, satellite night-lights, UPI value and more. We combine them into a purchasing-power score and income and spend estimates for each pincode. They are modelled estimates, not measured incomes.

How current is the data?

The model is refitted weekly and has to pass fixed sanity checks before anything changes on the map. Each source updates on its own schedule, which the Signals & data page lists.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The map is free without signing in. A free account lets you save locations, and the dashboard comes with a 7-day trial that needs no card.

Can I use my own store data?

Yes. Upload a CSV or Excel file of your stores in the workspace; we geocode them and score each one against its area. Your data stays in your company's account and is encrypted at rest.

Do you have an API?

Yes. Any account can create an API key for pincode-level exports; Growth plans and above get higher rate limits.
