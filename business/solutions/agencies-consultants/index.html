Your questions

What you ask, and where the answer is

Clients

How do we keep clients separate?

Each client is its own company with its own projects and data. Switch between them from the header.

Deliverables

What do we hand over?

Comparisons, PDF reports, read-only links and exports your client can open without a login.

Client data

Can we use a client's store data?

Upload it into that client's company only. It's encrypted and never mixed with another client's.

Budgets

How do we control spend per client?

Set a credit budget per client company, and allowances per person.

Purchasing power

3 signals
  • Purchasing Power IndexCore

    One number for an area's spending power, 100 = the regional average.

    Pincode · modelledBuilt from every signal below
  • Household income / monthCore

    Estimated monthly income of a typical household in the pincode.

    Pincode · modelledNSSO HCES, calibrated with state and district data
  • Household spend / monthCore

    Estimated monthly consumption spend of a typical household.

    Pincode · modelledNSSO HCES household spending survey

Banking & UPI

3 signals
  • Bank branches / lakh peopleLite

    How well served an area is by formal banking.

    DistrictRBI branch statistics
  • Bank deposits per personLite

    Savings held in banks, a proxy for accumulated wealth.

    DistrictRBI BSR deposits
  • UPI value per personLite

    How much people pay digitally, a live signal of everyday spending.

    DistrictPhonePe Pulse

Tax & economy

5 signals
  • Income-tax filers per personPro

    Share of people filing returns, a direct marker of formal income.

    StateCBDT annual report
  • MSMEs / lakh peopleLite

    Density of small businesses, a read on local enterprise.

    DistrictMSME Udyam registrations
  • Factories / lakh peoplePro

    Industrial employment base.

    StateASI factory counts
  • State output per personLite

    Net state domestic product per head, the wider economy around the area.

    StateState NSDP statistics
  • Cropping intensityPro

    How intensively farmland is used, useful for rural markets.

    StateMoSPI statistical year book

FAQ

Questions we hear

Can one wallet pay for several clients?

Yes. Link client companies to your account; credits are drawn from your wallet and usage is reported per client.

Do our clients need seats?

Seats are counted across your whole account. Client stakeholders can join as free viewers.

Can we white-label?

Not today. Talk to us about bulk data feeds for your own tools.

See your own market on the map

Tell us the cities and the decision you're making. We'll walk through it with you on the real data in about 20 minutes.
