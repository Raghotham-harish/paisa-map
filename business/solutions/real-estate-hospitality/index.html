  • Purchasing Power IndexCore

FAQ

Questions we hear

Is the property rate based on transactions?

No. It's a city-level estimate, labelled as such, and free on every plan. Use it as context, not as a valuation.

Does it work for hotels?

Yes, for reading the spending power of the surrounding area. It doesn't include tourism or occupancy data.

Can we share it with partners?

Yes. Generate a PDF report or a read-only project link, or add partners as free viewers.
