FAQ

Questions we hear

Can this be used for individual credit decisions?

It's an area-level signal: the same for everyone in a pincode. Use it alongside your own customer-level data and your model governance, not instead of them.

How do we get it into our models?

Export by pincode as CSV, Excel or JSON, or pull it with an API key. Growth and above get higher API limits.

Where do the banking signals come from?

RBI branch and deposit statistics, and PhonePe Pulse for UPI value. The signals page lists each source and level.
